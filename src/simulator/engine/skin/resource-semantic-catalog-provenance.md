## Algorithm and renderer boundary

Runtime random state belongs to each concrete `(owner generation, ParticleSystem component)`. The recovered auto/manual seed routines do not by themselves prove complete native construction timing or global allocation order. Canonical resource/path hashes and definition-global streams are forbidden. Shape 0/4/5/8/10 and no-Shape, module draw order, time/emission/capacity, parent order, Slide outer `n` versus ParticleSystem setup `g`, and renderer mode 0/1/4 are executed by the current native-semantic engine.

The engine emits immutable portable vertices, UVs, normals, colors and material-pass data. Its existing particle sort tuple and fixed low-stage publication are **not native mixed-renderer ordering**. Full bounds, camera uniforms, worker streams and ordinary/HUD consumption remain open; these algorithm/integration gaps cannot be assigned to GPU/driver exclusions.
